PostGIS
PostgreSQL vn l h qun tr c s d liu quan h (RDBMS).
Khi ci thm PostGIS, PostgreSQL tr thnh mt Spatial Database (c s d liu khng gian)  c th lu tr, truy vn, v phn tch d liu a l (geographic hoc geometric).
PostGIS thm vo PostgreSQL:
Kiu d liu khng gian, v d:
GEOMETRY (hnh hc phng)
GEOGRAPHY (d liu theo kinh /v )
Hm x l khng gian, nh:
ST_Distance(a, b)  tnh khong cch gia hai im.
ST_Area(polygon)  tnh din tch vng.
ST_Intersects(a, b)  kim tra hai vng c giao nhau khng.
ST_Buffer(point, distance)  to vng bao quanh im.
PostGIS thng c dng trong:
H thng bn  (GIS)  nh QGIS, GeoServer, MapServer.
ng dng nh v (location-based apps): tm qun gn nht, khong cch gia hai a im.
Phn tch a l: vng ph sng, khu vc ri ro, phn vng dn c.
Qun l h tng: ng x, mng in, cng rnh, vin thng.
POI:
POI l im quan tm hoc a im ng ch  trn bn , v d: qun c ph, trm xng, bnh vin, trng hc, nh hng, im du lch, v.v.
Mi POI c ta  khng gian (latitude, longitude) v thng tin m t i km.
POI thng l mt im (Point) trong d liu GIS (Geographic Information System) hoc PostGIS.
Google Maps Directions API:
Chc nng:
Tnh l trnh theo nhiu phng tin:  t (driving, c h tr kt xe), i b, xe p, phng tin cng cng (transit).
i qua nhiu im dng (waypoints) v chia l trnh thnh nhiu legs. (Lu : waypoint p dng cho driving/walking/cycling  khng p dng cho transit.)
Ti u th t im dng (t ng sp xp route  tng thi gian/ di ti u)  hin lm qua Routes API vi optimizeWaypointOrder=true.
ETAs c xt giao thng thc t: t departure_time (hin ti hoc tng lai) + traffic_model  nhn duration_in_traffic cho ch  li xe.
Nhiu cch ch nh im: a ch vn bn, Place ID, hoc ta  lat/lng.
Tham s iu chnh l trnh: trnh cao tc/thu ph/ph (avoid=highways|tolls|ferries), chn ngn ng/n v, ly ng polyline  v route trn bn , km steps (hng r) v fare cho transit.
Nhiu phng n tuyn (alternatives)  ngi dng so snh.
1. Kt hp im hi ngh / cng vic vo lch trnh
Dng PostGIS  lu v truy vn d liu POI, tnh khong cch t im hi ngh hoc cng vic n cc a im xung quanh bng ST_Distance hoc ST_DWithin.
Dng Google Maps Directions API  to l trnh di chuyn gia cc im (origin  hi ngh  POI  khch sn).
Dng PostGIS  lc POI theo bn knh (v d 2 km quanh a im hi ngh), sau  gi danh sch  vo Directions API  tnh tuyn ti u.
2. Ti u thi gian di chuyn v trnh gi cao im
Dng Google Maps Directions API (hoc Routes API) vi departure_time v traffic_model  tnh ETA c xt n giao thng thc t (duration_in_traffic).
Dng optimizeWaypointOrder=true  sp xp th t im dng hp l, gim tng thi gian di chuyn.
Dng PostGIS  xc nh cc POI gn nht trc khi gi yu cu n API, gip gim chi ph tnh ton.
3. Gi  lch trnh theo ma
Lu thm thuc tnh ma hoc iu kin thi tit cho tng POI (v d: summer_only, indoor, outdoor) hoc thm ct best_months hoc season trong bng a im.
Logic: Khi ngi dng chn I will travel in March  May, h thng u tin a im c best_months cha khong thi gian ny.
C th hin th thm L do gi  (v d: Ma hoa tam gic mch  H Giang hoc Thi im ln bin p  Ph Quc).
Dng PostGIS  lc POI ph hp theo ma v khu vc.
Dng Directions API  tnh tuyn di chuyn gia cc POI c chn (v d: chn im trong nh vo ma ma, im ngoi tri vo ma kh).
4. Gi  ba gi lch trnh (Tit kim  Tiu chun  Nng cao tri nghim)
Dng Google Maps Directions API vi alternatives=true  ly nhiu tuyn kh thi.
p dng tham s avoid=tolls cho gi tit kim, avoid=highways cho gi tri nghim.

5. Map c h tr phng tin di chuyn
Routes API v Directions API cung cp d liu chi tit cho cc phng tin:  t, i b, xe p, phng tin cng cng (mode=driving|walking|bicycling|transit).
Khi ngi dng m lch trnh, h thng hin th thng tin phng tin di chuyn (v d: tuyn xe but, tu in ngm, thi gian chuyn trm, khong cch i b gia cc chng).
PostgreSQL lu loi phng tin (transport_mode) cho tng chng, gip hin th ng nht gia h thng ni b v Google Maps.
6. Hin th thng tin y t v i s qun khi xem bn 
 mi im trong lch trnh (hotel, hi ngh, POI), hin th nt: Xem xung quanh.
Khi bm, m bottom sheet vi hai tab: Y t v i s qun.
Mi tab lit k cc im gn nht theo bn knh cu hnh (v d 13 km), xp theo khong cch v trng thi m ca.
Mi item c cc hnh ng: Gi/Website, Xem chi tit, Ch ng (m Google Maps deeplink).
C nt M c lp trn bn   bt overlay tt c im Y t/i s qun gn im  trong ch  Overview ca app.
Cng ngh:
Bc 1: Lu v truy vn d liu
Dng PostgreSQL + PostGIS  lu d liu y t v i s qun
Dng ST_DWithin  lc im gn, ST_Distance  tnh khong cch v sp xp.
Bc 2: B sung thng tin chi tit (gi m ca, lin h, website)

Bc 3: Hin th danh sch v bn  trong app
Dng Google Maps JavaScript API (hoc Maps SDK for Android/iOS)  hin th marker v overlay layer (GeoJSON).
Dng React / Android BottomSheet / iOS Sheet  hin th danh sch (bottom sheet) theo tab Y t v i s qun.
Bc 4: Ch ng t im hin ti n a im c chn
Dng Google Maps Directions API hoc deeplink (https://www.google.com/maps/dir/?api=1&destination=lat,lng)  m l trnh iu hng.
Bc 5: M lp bn  tng quan (Overview)
Dng GeoJSON layer t API ni b (PostGIS  GeoJSON)  overlay cc im y t/i s qun ln bn .
